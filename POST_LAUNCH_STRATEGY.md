## Phase 2: Growth Hacking (Week 2-4)

### ProductHunt Launch
**When:** Tuesday-Thursday, 6am-9am PST (peak hours)
**Steps:**
1. Create ProductHunt account
2. Add screenshots from your landing page
3. Write compelling tagline: "Batch screenshot upload to all AI platforms"
4. Respond to every comment in first 24 hours
5. Target: 500+ upvotes = top 5 product of the day

**Expected result:** 2,000-5,000 visitors, 500-1,000 installs

### HackerNews Post
- Submit to HackerNews: https://news.ycombinator.com/submit
- Headline: "SnapToAI – Batch screenshots to ChatGPT, Claude, Grok (free, client-side)"
- Wait for votes
- Target: 100+ upvotes = 5,000+ visitors

### Dev Community
- Dev.to: Write blog post about the problem you solved
- IndieHackers: Post launch
- Indiehackers (IH): Join daily thread with your product

## Phase 4: User Feedback Loop (Week 2+)

### Monitor & Respond
1. Check Chrome Store reviews daily (first 30 days critical)
2. Respond to EVERY review (thank positive, fix negative)
3. Join AI communities on Discord/Twitter
4. Ask users: "What would make SnapToAI 10x better?"

### Quick Wins to Implement
Listen for top 3 feature requests:
- Likely: "Add keyboard shortcut customization"
- Likely: "Save custom annotations as templates"
- Likely: "Drag-drop images into popup"

Implement fastest fix first. Push update within 1 week. Users see you care → more 5-star reviews.

---

## Phase 5: Scale & Monetization (Month 3+)

### Metric Targets
- **Week 1:** 100-500 installs
- **Week 2:** 500-2,000 installs (post-ProductHunt)
- **Month 1:** 5,000-10,000 installs
- **Month 3:** 50,000+ installs
- **Month 6:** 500,000+ installs

### If You Hit 10,000+ Users
Then consider:
1. **Error tracking** (Sentry) - understand where users struggle
2. **Analytics** (privacy-first only) - what features get used most
3. **Pro tier** (optional):
   - Unlimited downloads per month
   - Advanced templates
   - Priority support
   - Custom stickers
   - Price: $2-3/month = potential $20k-$30k MRR at 10K users

### But DO NOT add monetization until:
- ✅ 10,000+ active users
- ✅ 4.5+ star rating
- ✅ Users ask for it

---

## Phase 6: Long-Term Growth (Month 6+)

### Organic Growth Engine
1. **SEO:** "batch screenshot extension" + 54 languages = 100K search queries/month
2. **Word-of-mouth:** Users share with teammates (very viral for productivity tools)
3. **Integration partners:** Reach out to notion, linear, figma communities
4. **AI model mentions:** If ChatGPT team or Claude team mentions your tool = 10K+ installs overnight

### Company Formation (when you hit 100K users)
- Consider LLC
- Add Privacy Policy, Terms of Service
- Think about future monetization

## Your Competitive Advantage

vs. Competitors:
- ✅ 54-language support (they have 5-10)
- ✅ Works with ALL AI platforms (they support 1-2)
- ✅ Annotation tools built-in (they're separate)
- ✅ 100% client-side (they log data)
- ✅ Free forever (they have paywalls)

**Result:** You win on trust + features + speed = viral growth

---

## What NOT to Do

❌ Don't add tracking/analytics before 10K users (waste of time)
❌ Don't create a backend server (defeats privacy value prop)
❌ Don't add ads (kills user trust)
❌ Don't ignore reviews (they're your feedback)
❌ Don't delay launch for "perfect" features (ship now, iterate)

---
