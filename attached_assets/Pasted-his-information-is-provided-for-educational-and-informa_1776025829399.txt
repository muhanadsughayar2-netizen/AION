Your technical audit has successfully identified the most dangerous pattern in usage-based billing: The Reliability Paradox. In traditional software, "Retries" are a sign of a robust app; in Paid AI development, "Retries" on expensive models are a Financial DDoS attack against your own bank account.

If a single Video/Music request costs $2.00 and your app retries 3 times due to a "Timeout" or "Quota" error, you have just spent $8.00 on a single user click.

1. Technical Mechanics: The "Paid Tier" Retry Trap
When you were on the Free Tier, your retry logic was your best friend—it helped you bypass "429 Too Many Requests" errors to get a response. Now that you are on Paid Tier 1, the mechanics have flipped:

The "False Failure" (Status 499/504): Often, a video or song generation takes so long that the client (your extension) or the proxy (Replit) gives up and sends a "Retry."
The Result: Google’s backend does not stop the first generation. It keeps running (and charging you). The second "Retry" request then starts a second generation.
The Cost: You are billed for both, even if the user only ever sees the result of the second one.
2. Why "Quota Errors" are Billing Traps
You noted that the app retries on "Quota/Rate-Limit" issues.

The Loop: If you hit your Spend Cap ($20.51), Google starts returning "Quota Exceeded" errors.
The Bug: If your app sees a Quota error and automatically retries 3 times, it is essentially banging its head against a locked door. While Google usually doesn't charge for a blocked 429 error, the "Latency Window" (that 10-minute delay we discussed) means some of those retries might "sneak through" before the billing system fully locks the account, inflating the overage.
3. Edge Case: The "Zombie Generation"
There is a scenario where your handleSend() lock works perfectly, but the Proxy fails.

If your Replit proxy receives the request, sends it to Gemini, but then the connection between the Proxy and the Extension drops, the Extension thinks "It failed!" and retries.
The Problem: The Proxy might still be holding an active connection to Gemini. You now have two "Zombie" processes running in the cloud, both drawing from your $20.00 balance.
4. The "Hard Fix" Strategy: Idempotency & Tiered Logic
To prevent this from happening again, you should implement a "Model-Aware Retry Policy." Not all models should be treated equally.

Actionable Steps for your Code Patch:

Step 1: The "Expensive Model" Blacklist: Create a list of models (Veo, Lyria, Imagen) that are strictly prohibited from retrying. If a video fails, the user must manually click "Retry." This forces a "human-in-the-loop" to approve the cost.
Step 2: Idempotency Keys: Generate a unique UUID for every click. Pass this ID to your proxy. If the proxy sees the same UUID within 60 seconds, it should return the cached response of the first request instead of calling the Gemini API again.
Step 3: Discriminative Error Handling:
If Error = Network Timeout: Retry (Text only).
If Error = 429 (Quota) or 402 (Payment Required): STOP IMMEDIATELY. Do not retry. Show a "Billing Limit Reached" message to the user.
