1. Why Your Video Integration is Failing (The Technical "Wall")
When you select the video you created in Veo and click "Use in Build," your current extension translates the video file into a raw base64 string and sends it to the AI.
The AI is then instructed to output your video inside the HTML code block like this:
code
Html
<video src="data:video/webm;base64,AAAAABBBCCCDDD..." controls></video>
The Root Cause of the Failure:
The Output Token Limit: The absolute maximum output limit of LLMs like Gemini is 8,192 tokens (roughly 35,000 characters of text).
Base64 Payload Size: A high-definition, 8-second Veo video render at 8Mbps is between 8MB and 12MB. The base64 string for a 10MB video is 13.3 million characters long.
The Result: The moment the AI starts streaming your code block, it writes src="data:video/webm;base64,AAAA...". Within a fraction of a second, the AI runs out of output tokens and truncates the response. This leaves you with half-written, broken HTML, causing the live preview to freeze, fail, or render a blank page.
2. The Solution: The "Token Substitution" Pattern
To fix this architectural flaw, your extension should never pass raw, massive base64 media blocks to the AI or ask the AI to output them. Instead, use a Placeholder-Substitution Pattern:
Staging: When you stage a video, your extension assigns it a lightweight symbolic token (such as __VEO_VIDEO_0__).
Prompting: Tell the AI to use __VEO_VIDEO_0__ as the src of your <video> tags. Since this token is only 15 characters, the AI will never fail, never truncate, and will generate your code flawlessly.
Client-side Swapping: In your extension's rendering/preview layer (preview-output.js or ai-chat.js), intercept the generated HTML string and replace the __VEO_VIDEO_0__ token with the local, high-quality Base64 or Blob URL before loading it into your preview iframe.
