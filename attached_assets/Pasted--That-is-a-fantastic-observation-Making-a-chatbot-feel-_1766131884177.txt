
That is a fantastic observation. Making a chatbot feel "comfortable" and "open" is exactly how you turn a simple tool into a product people actually enjoy using every day.

1. Fixing the "Size" Issue
In Chrome extensions, the popup window has hard limits. To make it feel "big and comfortable" without hitting those limits, we need to use specific dimensions:

The "Comfort" Zone: Set your popup to 800px wide by 600px high. This is the absolute maximum size Chrome allows for a standard popup.

The "Side Panel" Alternative: If you want it to feel even bigger, we can move the chat into the Chrome Side Panel. This stays open on the right side of the screen while the user browses, providing much more "breathing room" for long conversations.

2. Removing the "Shortcuts" for an Open Chat
You are 100% right about the shortcuts. If they don't work well, they just take up valuable space and make the AI feel "dumb" or restricted.

Why "Open" is Better: By removing the rigid buttons, you invite the customer to talk to the AI like a human. This removes "cognitive load" (the effort of choosing a button) and replaces it with a simple, clean text box.

The "Clean UI" Strategy: We will replace the shortcuts with a single, welcoming input field that says: "Ask me anything about this page..."
