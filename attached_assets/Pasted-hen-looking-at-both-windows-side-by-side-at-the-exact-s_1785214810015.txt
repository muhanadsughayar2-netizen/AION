hen looking at both windows side-by-side at the exact same size, the core reason Aion feels uncomfortable—even with the same window dimensions and without removing any buttons—comes down to visual density, container nesting, and color contrast.

Here is the exact side-by-side breakdown of what is happening and how to fix it without losing a single button or feature:

1. "Box-in-Box" Nesting (Visual Claustrophobia)
The Difference: Gemini presents text directly on an open canvas. Aion puts the AI response inside a dark gray rounded card container, which sits inside another dark canvas.

Why it hurts: Putting text inside a border/box cuts off reading flow and makes the viewport feel tight and cramped.

The Fix: Remove the background card/border behind the AI response text. Let the text float directly on the main dark background just like standard chat interfaces do.

2. Neon Color Glare on Dark Backgrounds
The Difference: Gemini uses soft neutral tones. Aion uses high-saturation neon cyan/green text (Gemini 3 Flash, 1. Resize..., 2. Dismiss...) and bright green/teal buttons (Continue).

Why it hurts: High-contrast neon text on a pitch-black background causes chromatic aberration (eye glare/strain), forcing your eye muscles to work harder to focus on the text.

The Fix:

Change neon text/numbers to a soft off-white (#E2E8F0) or muted grayish-blue.

Use neon colors only for subtle hover states, not for primary text or numbers.

3. Header Height & Wasted Vertical Space
The Difference: Gemini’s chat area goes almost all the way to the top of the window. Aion’s header is thick, dark, and filled with a large purple banner, a bright green title, and top margins.

Why it hurts: It steals ~20% of your vertical reading screen, forcing you into a small "letterbox" viewing area.

The Fix:

Reduce top header padding from 24px to 8px–12px.

Make the purple billing banner smaller/inline or a tiny dismissible icon so the text area expands vertically.

4. Sidebar Stealing Horizontal Space
The Difference: In the second image, Aion's left "SCREENSHOT" panel takes up ~25% of the window width, pushing the actual chat into an off-center column. Gemini gives 100% of the window width to the conversation.

Why it hurts: On a narrow side-by-side screen, losing 25% of horizontal space makes chat lines wrap awkwardly fast.

The Fix: Implement an auto-collapse for the left screenshot sidebar when the window width is under a certain breakpoint (or show it as a tiny floating thumbnail badge).

5. Button Styling: From "Heavy Blocks" to "Subtle Icons"
(Keeping all features intact)

The Difference: Every button in Aion currently has a prominent background fill, bright outline, or distinct color block, making them all compete for attention.

Why it hurts: Your eye reads every button as an urgent "object" rather than passive tools.

The Fix:

Keep every single button (Vision, Image, Music, Build, Research, Search, etc.), but switch their default visual state to Ghost / Flat styling (transparent backgrounds with soft gray icons/text).

Only highlight a button with color when it is actively toggled on or hovered over.
