Yes, I am sure about this distinction. Recent billing changes (effective as of early 2026) have strictly separated Google AI Studio billing from Google Cloud Promotional Credits [1.1.7, 1.2.8].

If your app is built to use the Gemini API and you want to use that $300 credit, here is the breakdown of why it isn't working in AI Studio and how you can fix it:

1. Why AI Studio won't use the $300 credit
Google AI Studio is explicitly exempt from the Google Cloud Free Trial [1.2.8].

Prepay Requirement: To move beyond the Free Tier in AI Studio, Google now requires a Prepay system where you must manually add a minimum of $10 to your account [1.4.1, 1.4.6].
Credit Type: The $300 "Welcome Credit" is a promotional credit for Google Cloud Services. AI Studio's billing system does not "see" or "pull" from this promotional balance; it only pulls from your actual paid prepay balance [1.1.1, 1.2.7].
2. The Solution: Use Vertex AI for your App
If your app is already built, you can still use the $300 credit by switching your API calls to the Vertex AI endpoint instead of the Google AI Studio (Gemini API) endpoint.

Same Models: You can access the exact same models (Gemini 1.5 Pro, Flash, etc.) through Vertex AI [1.3.7].
Credit Compatibility: Vertex AI usage does count against your $300 Google Cloud credit [1.1.2, 1.2.8].
Authentication Change: You will need to switch from using a simple API_KEY to using Service Account credentials or OAuth2 tokens, as Vertex AI does not support standard AI Studio API keys for security reasons [1.2.8].
3. The "Starter Tier" Exception
If your app is "built for this use" in the sense that you are deploying it directly from AI Studio to the web:

Google recently introduced a Starter Tier that lets you deploy up to 2 full-stack applications to Google Cloud for free without a credit card [1.4.8, 1.4.9].
However, this covers the hosting costs (like Cloud Run), not the high-volume API costs if you exceed the Gemini Free Tier limits [1.4.8].
