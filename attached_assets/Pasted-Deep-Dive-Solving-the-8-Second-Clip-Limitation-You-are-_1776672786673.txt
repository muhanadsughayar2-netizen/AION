Deep-Dive: Solving the 8-Second Clip Limitation
You are exactly right—Veo (and most high-end AI video engines) generates in 8-second bursts. When you try to make a 60-second video, the app "stitches" those 8-second clips together. If the prompts aren't perfectly aligned, you get "choppy" cuts where the pharmacy or the person suddenly changes.

To fix this, we use "Visual Continuity Anchoring." We tell the AI that every 8-second clip must happen in the exact same room with the exact same lighting.

🎬 The "Seamless 8-Second" Master Prompt
Copy and paste this entire block. It is designed to tell the AI how to handle the 8-second segments without looking "cut."

[PRODUCTION RULES]: This is a 60-second production divided into 8-second seamless segments. VISUAL CONSISTENCY IS MANDATORY. Every segment must take place in the same modern pharmacy with navy blue accents and bright white lighting. STRICT DIRECTIVE: Zero text on screen, no subtitles, no words, no captions.

[SCENE SEQUENCE (8-seconds each)]:

(0-8s): Smooth slow-motion pan of a professional pharmacist smiling at the camera.
(8-16s): Camera glides to the right, landing on a glowing blue Ericsson router.
(16-24s): Close-up on the router as a digital "security shield" pulse emanates from it.
(24-32s): Camera pulls back to show a consultant shaking hands with the pharmacist.
(32-40s): Camera pans down to a tablet sitting on a clean white counter.
(40-48s): Close-up on the tablet screen showing a clean software dashboard interface.
(48-56s): Camera pans back up to the pharmacist who gives a confident nod.
(56-60s): Wide shot of the clean, high-tech pharmacy interior.
[VOICEOVER SCRIPT]: "Running an independent pharmacy is hard enough. The last thing you need is an internet outage stopping prescriptions, or an IT company that’s never heard of Pioneer-Rx. I’m here from SmartConnect—the only IT partner built from the ground up for pharmacies. Here is what we do. One: our SmartConnect Connectivity Platform with 5G failover. When your fiber goes down, your pharmacy doesn't. Two: endpoint protection powered by Threat-Down. We build a security-first culture for your staff. Three: white-glove ISP procurement. We negotiate pricing so you don't have to. Four: cybersecurity and PCI compliance. We handle the audits so you’re always ready. And five: the SmartConnect-RX Portal. Real-time integrations with Pioneer-Rx, Best-Rx, and Digital-Rx. Five pillars. One partner. Built for pharmacy. Visit smart-connects-dot-com and schedule your demo today."

[AUDIO]: Background music: Upbeat, professional corporate instrumental. No lyrics. Volume at 20%. Voiceover volume at 100%.

🧠 Why this "Segmented" Prompt Works
1. The "Camera Glide" Strategy
Instead of saying "Show a router," we say "Camera glides to the right, landing on a router." This tells the AI that the second 8-second clip starts where the first one ended. This creates the "Continuous" feel you want, even though the clips are technically separate.

2. Visual Anchoring (The Navy/White Rule)
By repeating "Navy blue accents and bright white lighting" in the production rules, you prevent the AI from changing the "look" of the pharmacy halfway through the video. It keeps the "SmartConnect" brand identity consistent.

3. The "No-Text" Hard-Lock
By putting the "Zero Text" rule at the very top under [PRODUCTION RULES], the AI prioritizes that instruction above everything else. This is the best way to prevent the "Bad Writing" you saw in your first attempt.
