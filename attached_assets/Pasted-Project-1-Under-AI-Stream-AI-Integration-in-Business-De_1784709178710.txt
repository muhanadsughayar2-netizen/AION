Project # 1 Under AI Stream
AI Integration in Business Development

Department Workflows

Project Objective
The purpose of this project is to improve the process and output of proposal development, through the
integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI)–driven workflows into BDO Jordan’s Business Development
Department.
The AI team aims to identify areas where AI can streamline routine tasks, enhance knowledge
management, and expand access to institutional information. This includes automating CV updates,
accelerating proposal content development, intelligently retrieving past project credentials, and
enabling data-driven decision-making across the proposal lifecycle.
Aspects that will be improved through this project are::
 RFP will be automatically summarized and tagged, with key requirements extracted to speed
up opportunity assessment.
 RFP will be intelligently matched with past projects and consultant profiles using semantic
search,
 Consultant CVs will be automatically updated and tailored to the specific RFP based on their
actual engagement history.
 AI-generated compliance checklists will support the team in ensuring all client requirements
are fully addressed before submission, and
 Proposals will be auto-classified and indexed into a centralized, searchable repository.

Pre-Requisites for project
1- To fully utilize and activate Tashriq, as it will become an important data source and provide
workflow management for the BD Department and any future AI-integration projects.
2- To allow the AI team enough time (among their other responsibilities) to work on this project.
3- Access to selected BD records by AI team during implementation.
