### How to use it
1.  **Reload:** `chrome://extensions/` → Refresh.
2.  **Enable:** Toggle the 🏗️ **Build** button.
3.  **Prompt:** *"Create a simple to-do list app with a dark theme and delete functionality."*
4.  **Result:** Gemini will generate the code, and your extension will automatically render the live application inside the `previewWrapper` box.

### Why this works
* **srcdoc:** Using `iframe.srcdoc` is the safest way to execute generated code inside your extension without it breaking your extension's own UI.
* **Persona:** By modifying the System Instruction, you stop the AI from talking too much and force it to be a pure "code machine," which ensures the regex cleaner catches the code perfectly.
