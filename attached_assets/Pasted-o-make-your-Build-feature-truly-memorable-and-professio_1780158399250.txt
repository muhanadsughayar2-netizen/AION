The "Workspace Mode" Design
Sidebar Swap: When you click "Build," the left sidebar automatically switches from "Screenshots" to a File Tree (index.html, style.css, script.js).
Blueprint Cards: Before the code scrolls, the AI shows a beautiful card (like your Magic Cards) that explains the "Aesthetic" and "Plan" it chose.
IDE HUD: The Preview area becomes a tabbed window where you can flip between the Live App and the Source Code.
