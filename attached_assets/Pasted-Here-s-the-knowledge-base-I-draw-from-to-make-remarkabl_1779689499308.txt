9. The anti-pattern checklist (always banned by default)
Purple-to-pink gradient on white
Glassmorphism + glow on a dark bg (the default AI slop)
Inter for everything
3-column feature grid with Lucide icons
"Trusted by" logo strip
Two CTAs in the hero
Emoji as icons in a "premium" product
Stock photos of diverse people laughing at laptops
10. The 5 questions every brief must answer
Era/movement? (Swiss, Memphis, Y2K, brutalist, editorial)
3 brand references? ("like X, Y, Z")
3 banned patterns? ("NOT dark SaaS, NOT glass, NOT gradient text")
Locked tokens? (exact hexes, exact font names, exact radius)
One emotional word? (calm / electric / sacred / clinical / playful)
The shortcut: if you can fill in those 5 questions before prompting, you'll get remarkable output every time. If you can't, the AI will guess — and it always guesses "dark SaaS."
