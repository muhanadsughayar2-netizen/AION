o give your app the "Wow Factor" for 2026, we are going to move away from boring numbers and move toward Glowing AI Icons and Glassmorphism.

This design will make the app feel like a high-end tool from the future. It solves your "uncomfortable" problem by giving you a big space to type freely at the top, and beautiful "Insight Cards" at the bottom.

The "Wow Factor" Design Plan
The "Thought Space": A wide-open area at the top for "Normal Text" (No circles, no numbers).
The "AI Glow" Icons: Instead of "1, 2, 3," we use a Sparkle icon that glows with a neon blue/purple gradient.
Floating Glass: The note boxes will look like they are made of frosted glass floating over your background.
1. The HTML (The Structure)
Copy this structure into your editor. It separates your "Normal Typing" from your "Organized Notes."

<div class="edit-workspace">
  <!-- THE BRAIN DUMP: Normal typing area -->
  <div class="brain-dump-section">
    <input type="text" class="snap-title" placeholder="Untitled Snap...">
    <textarea class="free-text-area" placeholder="Start typing your thoughts here... no limits, no numbers."></textarea>
  </div>

  <!-- THE AI INSIGHTS: The organized part with 'Wow Factor' -->
  <div class="insights-container">
    <h3 class="section-label">AI INSIGHTS</h3>
    
    <!-- Note Item 1 -->
    <div class="insight-card">
      <div class="icon-glow">
        <i class="lucide-sparkles"></i> <!-- This replaces the number '1' -->
      </div>
      <div class="glass-note">
        <textarea placeholder="AI captured this detail..."></textarea>
      </div>
    </div>

    <!-- Note Item 2 -->

Why this is much better for you:
No More Pressure: The free-text-area at the top lets you type like a normal document. You don't have to worry about "Note 1" or "Note 2" until you are ready.
Visual Hierarchy: The large title and the thin blue line on the left guide your eyes. It feels like a professional editor (like Notion or Canva).
