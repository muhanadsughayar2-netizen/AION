 Transform Your Screen into Actionable Intelligence SnapToAI is a high-performance productivity framework that bridges the gap between your browser and the power of Google Gemini. Stop manual data entry—start capturing context. SnapToAI allows you to capture high-fidelity visual data and process it through Custom AI Agents that you build and control.

Precision Capture Suite SnapToAI uses native browser-level capturing (PNG) to ensure the AI sees exactly what you see. No messy HTML scraping—just pure visual accuracy.

SNAP: Capture your immediate viewport in one click.
SNIP: Precision-select any specific area, chart, or code block.
FULL PAGE: Automatically scroll and stitch entire web pages into one image.
Pro Shortcuts: Trigger captures instantly with Ctrl+Shift+S (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+S (Mac).
 Fully Customizable "Magic Agents" SnapToAI provides a blank canvas for your AI workflows. You are the architect of your AI.

Build Your Own Library: Create specialized personas for your specific job (Coding, Research, Translation, etc.).
Custom Prompt Templates: Define exactly how the AI should behave (e.g., "Summarize this chart" or "Explain this error code").
Name & Deploy: Save your agents and switch between them instantly to match your current task.
Unlimited Creation: There is no limit to the number of custom agents you can create and manage.
 Direct-to-Google Architecture (BYOK) SnapToAI is a Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) extension, ensuring maximum privacy and cost-efficiency.

No Middleman: Your screenshots are sent directly from your browser to the Google Gemini API. We never see your data.
Full Transparency: Use your own Gemini API key to stay in control of your usage and costs.
The $300 Advantage: We provide a step-by-step guide on how new Google Cloud users can activate their $300 free credit tier from Google to power their SnapToAI experience.
Privacy-First & Local-Only We prioritize your data security with a Zero-Persistence policy:

Session-Only Memory: Your capture queue (up to 9 images) is stored in temporary browser memory. Closing Chrome wipes the images clean.
No Background Tracking: SnapToAI only functions when you click the icon or use the shortcut.
Local Storage: Your API keys and custom agent prompts are stored locally on your device, not on our servers.
 Technical Mechanics & Permissions Disclosure SnapToAI requires the following permissions to function as a high-performance productivity tool:

activeTab: To capture the screen of your currently active page.
storage: To securely save your custom agent definitions and API preferences on your device.
scripting: To enable the optional "One-Click Upload" feature to AI chat interfaces.
clipboardWrite: To allow you to instantly copy AI-generated insights for use in other apps.
Usage Notes

API Requirement: An active Google Gemini API key is required to use the AI analysis features.
Manual Setup: Users create their own agents and prompts; the extension provides the framework, while the user provides the instructions.
Reversibility: You can modify your agents, delete your history, or remove the extension at any time with a single click.
