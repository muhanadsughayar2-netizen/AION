Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 22, 2024

Developer: Muhanad Sughayar
Location: Amman, Jordan

At SnapToAI, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle your data when you use the SnapToAI Chrome Extension.

1. Single Purpose

SnapToAI is designed for the single purpose of allowing users to capture screenshots of webpage content and interact with an integrated AI to analyze, summarize, or explain that visual information through a chat interface.

2. Information We Collect

To provide our service and manage your 30-day trial and subscription, we collect the following data:

  • Authentication Reference: We collect an encrypted hash reference of your API key. We do NOT see or store your actual API keys; we only store a non-reversible cryptographic hash to track trial usage and prevent abuse.
  • IP Address: We collect your IP address solely for the purpose of controlling trial abuse and ensuring the security of our services.
  • Website Content: When you capture a screenshot, the extension processes the visible area of your browser. This image/text is sent to our AI provider (Google Gemini) for analysis.
  • Personal Communications: We collect the text of the chat messages you send to the AI to generate responses and maintain your interaction history.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data strictly for the following purposes:

  • To provide the core functionality of screenshot analysis and AI chatting.
  • To manage your account and identify your trial/subscription status.
  • To improve the accuracy and performance of the AI responses.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

  • Payments: If you purchase a subscription, financial transactions are handled securely by our payment processor. We do not see, collect, or store your credit card or financial details.
  • AI Processing: Your captured screenshots and chat text are sent to a third-party AI provider (such as OpenAI or Google Gemini) via an encrypted API solely to generate a response.
  • No Sale of Data: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information only as long as necessary to provide the service. You may request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email provided below.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS encryption, to protect your data during transmission and processing.
