# SnapToAI - Chrome Web Store Submission Guide

## STEP 1: Create Developer Account (One-time, $5)
1. Go to: https://chrome.google.com/webstore/developer/dashboard
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Pay $5 one-time fee
4. Verify email
5. **Done** - you now have a developer account

---

## STEP 2: Upload Extension to Chrome Web Store

### What to Upload
Go to your developer dashboard → "New Item" → Upload the `flow/` folder

**Folder to upload:** The `flow/` folder (contains manifest.json, all files)

## STEP 4: Publish
1. Click "Submit for Review"
2. Wait 24-48 hours for approval
3. Once approved, your extension goes LIVE on Chrome Web Store
4. URL will be: `https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/snaptoai/[YOUR_STORE_ID]`
