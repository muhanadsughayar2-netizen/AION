🚀 Quick Start

1

Click the extension icon

Find the SnapToAI icon in your browser toolbar. Click it to open the capture menu.

2

Choose your capture mode

Snap for quick screenshots, Snip to crop specific areas, or Full Page for entire pages.

3

Send to AI or download

Upload directly to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, or any AI. Or download as PNG/PDF.

💡 Pro Tips

⌨️

Keyboard shortcut: Press Ctrl+Shift+S (or Cmd+Shift+S on Mac) to capture instantly without opening the popup.

📚

Queue up to 10 screenshots: Capture multiple images before uploading. Perfect for tutorials and bug reports.

🎨

Annotate before sending: Add highlights, arrows, and text to your screenshots before sharing with AI.

🔒

100% Private: All processing happens locally in your browser. No data is sent to any server (except when you choose to use AI).
