Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 02, 2026

Overview

SnapToAI is built with a privacy-first philosophy. Most of your data stays exactly where it belongs: on your device. This policy explains what data we collect to provide our services.

Data Usage Disclosure

In compliance with the Chrome Web Store data usage policies, we disclose the following collection practices:

Personally Identifiable Information Account details such as email address used for subscription management via Gumroad.
Authentication Information API Keys (like Gemini) and License Keys are stored securely in your browser's local storage to enable AI features.
Personal Communications The content of your AI chats and prompts processed by the Gemini engine.
Website Content Screenshots, snippets, and page text that you explicitly choose to capture and analyze.

How We Use Your Data

  • Service Provision: To capture screenshots and process them with AI as requested by you.
  • Subscription Management: To verify your Pro status via Gumroad.
  • Improvements: To understand how the extension is performing (via anonymous telemetry if enabled).

Data Security

Your screenshots are stored in your browser's session storage and are deleted when the session ends or when you clear your queue. We do not maintain a central database of your captured images.
