# 🎨 Professional Annotation Tools - Complete Rebuild

## 🚀 What Changed (November 21, 2025)

The annotation tools have been **completely rebuilt from scratch** with professional-grade features that solve real problems when sending screenshots to AI platforms. The old primitive tools (weak rectangle, broken blur) have been replaced with **WOW-FACTOR** features used by professionals.

---

## ✨ **NEW PROFESSIONAL TOOLS**

### 1. **✨ Smart Highlight Brush**
**What it does:** Draw freeform glowing strokes to spotlight important areas

**Perfect for:** "Look at THIS button" or "This error message is important"

**How to use:**
- Click the ✨ tool
- Draw freely over areas you want AI to focus on
- Glowing neon effect with 20px shadow makes it impossible to miss
- Semi-transparent so content remains visible

**Why it's better:** Instead of a useless rectangle, you get a professional highlight that guides AI attention exactly where you want it.

---

### 2. **🎯 Spotlight Mode (PRESENTATION EFFECT)**
**What it does:** Brightens one area, dims everything else (like PowerPoint spotlight)

**Perfect for:** "Only look at this section, ignore the rest"

**How to use:**
- Click the 🎯 tool
- Drag a rectangle around the important area
- Everything outside gets dimmed 65%
- Spotlight area has glowing cyan border

**Why it's WOW:** This is the feature that makes people say "WHOA!" - it looks like a professional presentation. AI sees exactly what you want it to see.

---

### 3. **🔒 Redaction Tool (PRIVACY PROTECTION)**
**What it does:** Pixelates sensitive information so you can safely share screenshots

**Perfect for:** Hiding passwords, emails, credit cards, personal info

**How to use:**
- Click the 🔒 tool
- Drag over sensitive areas
- Gets pixelated (15px blocks) with red border
- Impossible to read but you know it was redacted

**Why it matters:** Users can now send bug reports without exposing private data. This is a CRITICAL feature for real-world use.

---

### 4. **🔢 Numbered Callouts (STEP-BY-STEP)**
**What it does:** Adds sequential markers (1, 2, 3...) with labels

**Perfect for:** "First do this, then this, then this"

**How to use:**
- Click the 🔢 tool
- Click where you want marker #1
- Type label (e.g., "Click here")
- Repeat for steps 2, 3, etc.

**Features:**
- Large glowing circles with numbers
- Text label below each marker
- Perfect for tutorials and bug reports

**Why it's useful:** AI can follow multi-step instructions in the correct order. Way better than trying to describe "click the third button from the left"

---

### 5. **🏷️ Quick Stickers (ONE-CLICK LABELS)**
**What it does:** Pre-made labels you can slap on screenshots instantly

**Available stickers:**
- 🐛 **BUG** - Mark bugs/errors
- ⚠️ **IMPORTANT** - Highlight critical info
- 👉 **FOCUS HERE** - Direct AI attention
- ✅ **GOOD** - Show correct behavior
- ❌ **WRONG** - Show incorrect behavior

**How to use:**
- Click any sticker button
- It appears centered on screenshot
- Can add multiple stickers

**Why it's fast:** Instead of typing "this is a bug" every time, one click applies a professional label. Saves tons of time.

---

### 6. **✏️ Text Tool (IMPROVED)**
**What it does:** Add custom text anywhere on screenshot

**Improvements:**
- Larger, bolder font (28px)
- Black outline for readability on any background
- Clean input field

**Perfect for:** Custom annotations that aren't covered by stickers
