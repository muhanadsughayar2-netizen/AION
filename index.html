🚀 Installation Instructions

Open Chrome Extensions
Navigate to chrome://extensions/ in your browser
Enable Developer Mode
Toggle the "Developer mode" switch in the top-right corner
Load Unpacked Extension
Click "Load unpacked" and select the flow folder
Start Capturing!
Click the Flow icon or press Ctrl+Shift+S to capture screenshots

📖 How to Use

Capture Screenshots

• Press Ctrl+Shift+S (Mac: Cmd+Shift+S) anywhere to capture
• Or click the Flow extension icon and click the glowing cyan orb
• Screenshots are auto-saved and copied to clipboard

Upload to AI Platforms

• Navigate to ChatGPT, Claude, or Grok
• Click the Flow icon to open the popup
• Click the glowing orb to batch upload all snapshots
• Uploads happen with 1.4s delay between each file

Manage Snapshots

• View thumbnails in the popup (max 10 snapshots)
• Click "Clear All" to remove all snapshots
• Oldest snapshots auto-removed when limit reached (FIFO)
