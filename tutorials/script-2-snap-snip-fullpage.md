# Tutorial 2: Snap, Snip & Full Page Capture
## HeyGen Video Script

**Estimated Duration:** 4-5 minutes

---

### INTRO (15 seconds)

"Welcome back! In this tutorial, I'll show you the three powerful capture modes in SnapToAI - Snap, Snip, and Full Page. You'll learn how to grab anything on your screen, combine multiple screenshots, and flip them to AI for instant analysis. Let's dive in."

---

### SECTION 1: Snap - Full Screenshot Capture (45 seconds)

"Let's start with Snap - the fastest way to capture your entire screen.

Step 1: Navigate to any webpage you want to capture. Let's say you're looking at a stock chart on TradingView.

Step 2: Click the SnapToAI icon in your toolbar.

Step 3: Hit the big 'Snap' button - the camera icon.

That's it. SnapToAI instantly captures everything visible on your screen and saves it to your queue. You'll see a small preview appear showing your captured screenshot.

You can snap up to 9 screenshots in a row. Each one gets added to your queue automatically. Think of it like loading bullets into a magazine - snap, snap, snap - then fire them all at the AI at once."

---

### SECTION 2: Snip - Precise Region Capture (60 seconds)

"Sometimes you don't need the whole screen. Maybe you just want one chart, one table, or one paragraph. That's where Snip comes in.

Step 1: Click the SnapToAI icon.

Step 2: Hit the 'Snip' button - the scissors icon.

Step 3: Your screen will dim slightly. Now click and drag to draw a rectangle around exactly what you want to capture.

Step 4: Release the mouse. SnapToAI captures only the area you selected and saves it to your queue.

This is perfect for grabbing specific sections of a financial report, a single chart from a dashboard, or a particular part of a webpage. You get exactly what you need - nothing more, nothing less.

Pro tip: Use Snip when you want the AI to focus on one specific thing. The less noise in your screenshot, the better the AI analysis will be."

---

### SECTION 3: Full Page Capture (60 seconds)

"Now here's something really powerful. What if you need to capture an entire webpage - not just what's visible, but everything from top to bottom? That's Full Page capture.

Step 1: Click the SnapToAI icon.

Step 2: Hit the 'Full Page' button.

Step 3: Watch as SnapToAI automatically scrolls through the entire page from top to bottom. You'll actually see it scrolling - it's capturing everything as it goes.

Step 4: When it reaches the bottom, SnapToAI stitches all those captures into one long, continuous image and saves it to your queue.

This is a game-changer for financial analysis. Imagine capturing an entire balance sheet, an entire income statement, or a full earnings report - all in one screenshot. No more scrolling up and down trying to piece things together. One click, one image, complete data."

---

### SECTION 4: Combining Multiple Screenshots (60 seconds)

"Here's where SnapToAI really shines. You can combine multiple screenshots and send them all to the AI at once.

Let's walk through a real example. Say you want to analyze a company's financials:

Snap 1: Go to the company's stock chart - hit Snap.
Snap 2: Go to the balance sheet page - hit Full Page.
Snap 3: Go to the income statement - hit Full Page.
Snap 4: Go to the cash flow statement - hit Full Page.

Now you have 4 screenshots in your queue, each showing different financial data.

You can reorder them by dragging and dropping. You can delete any you don't need. You can even select multiple screenshots at once."

---

### SECTION 5: Sending Screenshots to AI for Analysis (60 seconds)

"Now let's flip these screenshots to AI for analysis. You have two powerful options:

Option 1 - Built-in AI Analysis:
Click the 'AI' button in SnapToAI. This opens our built-in AI Chat powered by Google Gemini. Select one or more screenshots from your queue - just click on them. Then type your question: 'Analyze this company's financial health' or 'What does this chart tell me?' Hit Send, and the AI analyzes all your selected screenshots together, giving you a comprehensive response.

Option 2 - Send to External AI:
Click the 'Upload' button. SnapToAI switches to Upload Mode. Now go to any AI platform - ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Gemini - and click the upload area. SnapToAI automatically injects all your screenshots into the file upload. All of them, at once.

This means you can send 5, 6, 7 screenshots to ChatGPT in a single upload. No more dragging files one by one. No more saving screenshots to your desktop first. It's instant."

---

### OUTRO (15 seconds)

"That's Snap, Snip, and Full Page - three capture modes that let you grab anything on your screen and flip it to AI in seconds. In the next tutorial, I'll show you how to create Magic AI Prompts that turn SnapToAI into a powerful stock analysis tool. See you there!"
