### SECTION 3: Technical Analysis Prompt (90 seconds)

"Let's create a Technical Analysis prompt. This is for when you snap a stock chart and want instant technical analysis.

Name it: 'TA Pro'
Emoji: Pick the chart emoji

Here's the prompt - and this is the key to getting amazing results:

'You are a senior technical analyst with 20 years of experience. Analyze this stock chart with extreme precision. Identify and report:

1. TREND: Current trend direction, strength, and duration. Is it bullish, bearish, or consolidating?
2. SUPPORT & RESISTANCE: Key price levels where the stock has bounced or been rejected. Give exact numbers.
3. PATTERNS: Any chart patterns visible - head and shoulders, double tops, triangles, flags, wedges.
4. INDICATORS: If visible, analyze RSI, MACD, moving averages, volume, Bollinger Bands.
5. VOLUME: Is volume confirming the trend? Any divergences?
6. TIMEFRAME: What does this chart's timeframe tell us about short-term vs long-term outlook?
7. TRADE SETUP: Based on everything above, give a specific trade idea with entry, stop-loss, and target prices.
8. RISK RATING: Rate the setup from 1 to 10 for risk-reward quality.

Be decisive. Give specific numbers. No vague answers.'

Now here's a pro tip: for the best technical analysis, capture the same stock at different timeframes. Snap the daily chart, snap the weekly chart, snap the 4-hour chart. Select all three screenshots, then click your TA Pro button. The AI will analyze all timeframes together and give you a much more complete picture. This is called multi-timeframe analysis, and it's what professional traders do."

### SECTION 4: Fundamental Analysis Prompt (90 seconds)

"Now let's create a Fundamental Analysis prompt. This one is incredibly powerful because we're going to use Full Page capture to grab entire financial statements.

Name it: 'Fundamentals'
Emoji: Pick the money or bank emoji

Here's the prompt:

'You are a senior financial analyst and CFA charterholder. Analyze the financial data in these screenshots with the precision of a Wall Street professional. Evaluate:

1. REVENUE: Revenue trend, growth rate, and consistency over the periods shown.
2. PROFITABILITY: Gross margin, operating margin, net margin. Are they improving or declining?
3. EARNINGS: EPS trend, earnings quality, any one-time items distorting the numbers.
4. BALANCE SHEET HEALTH: Debt-to-equity ratio, current ratio, cash position. Can this company survive a downturn?
5. CASH FLOW: Operating cash flow vs net income. Is the company generating real cash or just paper profits?
6. RED FLAGS: Any warning signs - declining margins, rising debt, negative cash flow, accounting irregularities.
7. COMPETITIVE POSITION: Based on the numbers, how strong is this company's market position?
8. VALUATION: Based on the data shown, does this company appear overvalued, fairly valued, or undervalued?
9. VERDICT: Would you buy, hold, or sell? Give a confidence rating from 1 to 10.

Present your analysis like a professional research report. Be specific with numbers and percentages.'

Now here's how to use this like a pro:

Step 1: Go to a financial site like Yahoo Finance or Macrotrends.
Step 2: Navigate to the company's Income Statement page. Hit Full Page to capture the entire thing.
Step 3: Go to the Balance Sheet page. Hit Full Page again.
Step 4: Go to the Cash Flow Statement page. Hit Full Page one more time.

Now you have three full financial statements in your queue. Select all three, click your Fundamentals button, and watch the AI deliver a complete financial analysis that would take a human analyst hours to produce. You just did it in seconds."

2. TECHNICAL OUTLOOK: Chart patterns, trends, key levels, momentum.

### SECTION 6: Tips for Creating Perfect Prompts (45 seconds)

"Let me share some tips for creating prompts that get amazing results:

Tip 1: Be specific. Instead of saying 'analyze this', tell the AI exactly what to look for. The more detail in your prompt, the better the analysis.

Tip 2: Assign a role. Start with 'You are a senior analyst' or 'You are a CFA charterholder'. This makes the AI perform at a higher level.

Tip 3: Use numbered lists. When you structure your prompt with numbered items, the AI gives you organized, structured responses.

Tip 4: Ask for specific outputs. Say 'give exact numbers', 'rate from 1 to 10', 'give a buy or sell verdict'. This forces the AI to be decisive instead of vague.

Tip 5: Combine screenshots. The more context you give the AI, the better the analysis. A chart plus a balance sheet gives much better results than either one alone.

You can create up to 15 Magic Prompts. Build a toolkit that matches your workflow - maybe one for crypto analysis, one for real estate, one for competitor research. The possibilities are endless."
