# Tutorial 1: Getting Started with SnapToAI
## HeyGen Video Script

**Estimated Duration:** 3-4 minutes

---

### INTRO (15 seconds)

"Hey! Welcome to SnapToAI - the Chrome extension that lets you snap screenshots and flip them straight to AI for instant analysis. In this tutorial, I'll show you how to get set up in under two minutes - and how to get $300 in FREE credits from Google to power your AI. Let's go."

---

### SECTION 1: Why SnapToAI? (30 seconds)

"Before we start, let me tell you why SnapToAI is a game-changer. Most people copy and paste text into AI chatbots. But what about charts, dashboards, financial reports, or web designs? You can't copy-paste those.

SnapToAI lets you capture anything on your screen - snap it, snip it, flip it to AI - and get instant, intelligent analysis. Whether it's a stock chart, a balance sheet, a website design, or a homework problem, SnapToAI handles it all.

And here's the best part - Google gives you $300 in free cloud credits when you sign up. That's enough for extensive AI analysis without paying anything. No other tool gives you this kind of value."

---

### SECTION 2: Getting Your Google Gemini API Key (60 seconds)

"Alright, let's get your free API key. This is what powers the AI brain inside SnapToAI.

Step 1: Open your browser and go to aistudio.google.com.

Step 2: Sign in with your Google account. Any Gmail account works.

Step 3: Once you're in, click on 'Get API Key' in the left sidebar.

Step 4: Click 'Create API Key'. Google will generate a unique key for you.

Step 5: Copy that API key. Keep it safe - this is your personal key.

That's it. You now have access to Google's most powerful AI model - Gemini - and $300 in free credits from Google Cloud to use it."

---

### SECTION 3: Setting Up Your API Key in SnapToAI (45 seconds)

"Now let's connect your API key to SnapToAI.

Step 1: Click the SnapToAI icon in your Chrome toolbar. That's the little camera icon.

Step 2: Click 'API Setup' in the top area of the popup.

Step 3: You'll see a field to enter your Gemini API Key. Paste your API key right there.

Step 4: Click Save.

Done! SnapToAI is now connected to Google's AI. You're ready to start analyzing anything on your screen."

---

### SECTION 4: Upgrading to Unlimited Access (45 seconds)

"Now, let's talk about getting the most out of your setup.

With the free tier, Google gives you a generous amount of requests per minute. But if you're a power user - maybe you're analyzing dozens of stock charts or processing financial reports all day - you might want more speed.

Here's how to upgrade:

Step 1: Go to console.cloud.google.com.

Step 2: Navigate to 'Billing' and set up a billing account. Don't worry - Google gives you $300 in free credits, so you won't be charged until those run out.

Step 3: Once billing is enabled, your API limits are automatically increased. You get faster responses, more requests per minute, and priority access.

The $300 free credit from Google means you can use SnapToAI's AI features extensively without paying a single dollar. Most users never even use half of it."

---

### SECTION 5: Why We're So Competitive (30 seconds)

"Let me explain why SnapToAI stands out from everything else on the market.

First - $300 in free Google Cloud credits. No other screenshot tool gives you this. ChatGPT charges $20 a month. Claude charges $20 a month. With SnapToAI and Google's free credits, you get extensive AI analysis at no cost.

Second - it's built right into your browser. No switching tabs, no uploading files, no copy-pasting. Snap, snip, flip - done.

Third - your data stays private. Everything runs in your browser. We don't store your screenshots on any server. Your images go directly from your screen to Google's AI and back to you.

Fourth - it works with major AI platforms. ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Gemini - upload your screenshots to them with one click."
